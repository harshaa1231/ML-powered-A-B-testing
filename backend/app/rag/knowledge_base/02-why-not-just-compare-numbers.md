# Why Can't I Just Compare the Raw Numbers?

Say Version A got 100 conversions and Version B got 110. B looks better, right? Not necessarily — that 10-conversion gap could just be random noise, the same way some days are busier than others for no particular reason.

A/B testing uses statistics to answer one specific question: **"Is this difference real, or could it just be luck?"** That's what separates a data-driven decision from a gut call. Your gut might say B is better; the math might say "we can't be sure yet — you need more data."

This is why every A/B test result should be reported with a measure of statistical confidence (a p-value or confidence interval), not just the raw difference in numbers.
